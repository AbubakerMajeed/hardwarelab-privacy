Advertising & Tracking

Hardware Lab uses Google AdMob to display ads to free users. AdMob may use your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to show personalised ads and measure ad performance.

When you first open the app, iOS will ask: "Allow Hardware Lab to track your activity?"

  • Allow — personalised ads are shown (helps keep the app free)
  • Ask Not to Track — non-personalised ads are shown instead

You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

⚡ Go Pro — Zero Tracking, Zero Ads

Upgrade to Hardware Lab Pro ($4.99, one-time) and we immediately stop all ad SDKs for your session. No IDFA access. No AdMob. No tracking of any kind.
